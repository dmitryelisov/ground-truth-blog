Ground Truth is the engineering publication by Teramind Engineering.

Most of what we publish starts as an internal AMA. Every couple of weeks, one of our engineers walks the team through something they built, broke or fixed. We write up the parts that hold outside the room.

We write about three things:

- **AI-native development:** how we actually build with AI, including cost, review and the verification it still needs.
- **Distributed systems:** the backend decisions and what they cost us.
- **Security engineering:** building systems that produce evidence you can defend.

Each article is written in the name of the engineer who gave the talk. What we leave out: customer names, infrastructure specifics, and anything that would hand an attacker a map.
